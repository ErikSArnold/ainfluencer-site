Smart routing

The DMs that pay you get answered first.

AInfluencer classifies every message the moment it lands and ranks it by value and urgency. Money messages jump the queue, routine ones get handled for you, and nothing important gets buried.

1 · Classify

Sorts by intent: brand deal, collab, fan question, fan love, sensitive, spam — or your own custom categories.

2 · Prioritize

Scores urgency using the category, the sender's history, follower status and time-sensitive words like dates and deadlines.

3 · Route

Applies your rule: notify, draft, auto-reply, assign to a teammate, or hold — per category, per account.

Default rules — change any of them
Message type Priority How AInfluencer handles it
Brand deals & sponsorships Urgent Instant alert, reply with a series of curated questions, never auto-sent
Sensitive or personal Urgent Held for you only — no AI reply, flagged at the top of your DM Inbox
Collab requests High Draft in your voice for review, or assign to your manager
Negative comments & criticism High Highlighted and flagged for you with a suggested response — never auto-replied
Fan questions (links, products, schedule) Normal Autopilot answers from your saved answers and links
Fan love & compliments Normal Quick thank-you or voice note, or batched for your daily catch-up
Comments on your posts Normal Public reply in your voice; buying questions get the link by DM; negative ones flagged; hate hidden
Spam & scams Low Filtered out, suspicious links flagged, never answered

Add your own categories — merch orders, event bookings, podcast invites — and give each one a priority, a handling rule and an owner on your team.
