Pricing questions

Do unused replies roll over?

On paid plans, unused included replies roll over for one month, up to one month's allowance.

Will I be charged by surprise?

No. Overage is off until you turn it on, and you set a monthly cap. We email you at 80% and 100% of your bank.

Can I change plans any time?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated; downgrades take effect at the next billing date.
